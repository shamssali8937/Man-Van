## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup & Optimization (Priority 1)

Google Business Profile is the primary ranking signal for local map pack searches (e.g. *"man and van London"*, *"removals near me"*).

### 1.1 Category Configuration
* **Primary Category**: `Moving company` (mandatory — provides highest algorithmic relevance).
* **Secondary / Additional Categories**:
  * `Furniture removal service`
  * `Commercial mover`
  * `Waste management service` (apply only if separate clearance service is actively advertised; ensures compliance with Environment Agency licensing)

### 1.2 Business Model & Service Areas
Because AB Removals operates as a direct transport and dispatch service covering multiple regions, set up your profile as a **Service Area Business (SAB)**:
* Hide your residential/depot street address if customers do not visit on-site.
* Add your **Service Areas** using the 30 primary UK cities and regions (London, Greater London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol, Reading, Milton Keynes, etc.).
* Set opening hours: `Mon – Sun: 07:00 – 20:00` (or 24 hours if emergency/commercial overnight relocations are staffed).

### 1.3 Services List Configuration
Under GBP **Edit Services**, configure the exact 6 core service offerings:
1. **House Removals**
2. **Man and Van Services**
3. **Furniture Collection and Delivery**
4. **Office and Commercial Removals**
5. **Furniture Dismantling and Assembly**
6. **Waste Removal and Property Clearance**

### 1.4 High-Impact Visual Assets (No Stock Photos)
Upload genuine, geo-tagged photography to stand out against spam listings:
* **The Van**: High-resolution photos of the 3.5T Luton Box Van showing the AB Removals branding, registration plate, and hydraulic tail lift in action.
* **The Crew**: Photos of the loading team wearing clean branded uniforms, carrying sofas with straps, and laying protective floor runners.
* **Equipment**: Quilted removal blankets, sack barrows, piano dollies, and interior strap rails.

### 1.5 Review Acquisition Process
1. Generate your short Google Review link: In GBP Manager &rarr; **Ask for reviews** &rarr; copy `https://g.page/r/.../review`.
2. Send this link via WhatsApp or SMS immediately after a move completes while customer satisfaction is highest:
   > *"Hi [Name], thank you for trusting AB Removals with your move today! If you have 60 seconds, could you leave us a quick review on Google? It really helps our local family business: [Link]"*
3. Always respond to every review (both positive and negative) within 24 hours using professional, polite UK English.

## 4. White-Hat Backlink Acquisition Strategy (Priority 4)

Search engines value links from contextual, local, and industry-relevant UK websites.

### 4.1 Local Estate & Letting Agents
* Approach independent estate agents and student letting agents in your primary cities (London, Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds).
* Offer their tenants an exclusive 5–10% moving discount or provide a printable "Moving Day Checklist" branded with your details for their welcome packs.
* Request a link on their "Recommended Local Services" or "Tenant Resources" website page.

### 4.2 Self-Storage Facilities
* Storage facilities (e.g. Big Yellow, Safestore, Lok'nStore, and independent local container yards) constantly receive requests for van and driver transport.
* Leave business cards at their front reception desk and request inclusion on their approved removal contractors list and local facility web pages.

### 4.3 Environment Agency Accreditation
* Maintain active registration on the public Environment Agency waste register: `https://environment.data.gov.uk/public-register/view/search-waste-carriers-brokers`.
* Link to your official public register entry from your site's About and Clearance pages to prove legitimacy.

### 4.4 Local Sponsorship & Community News
* Sponsor a local youth sports team, charity community event, or neighborhood cleanup in East London / Home Counties.
* Local club websites and community news portals typically feature a do-follow link to sponsors.
